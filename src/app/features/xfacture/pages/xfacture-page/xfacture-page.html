XFacture invoice exports with X-Facture

Turn the invoice you already create in X-Facture into structured CII or UBL XML directly in your browser. No account server, database or Gotenberg service is required for these local exports.

Create an invoice Open invoices
CII

Cross Industry Invoice

Generate a CII XML representation from your invoice data. This is the structured XML layer used by formats such as ZUGFeRD / Factur-X.

UBL

Universal Business Language

Generate a UBL 2.1 invoice XML representation suitable as a foundation for European structured invoicing workflows.

What happens when you export?

  1. Your invoice remains the single source of truth inside X-Facture.
  2. The local structured-invoice engine maps that data to CII or UBL XML.
  3. The XML file is generated and downloaded in your browser.
  4. Server-side PDF/A-3 packaging and network exchange can be added separately as the X-Facture platform evolves.

XML export is a structured data feature. Country-specific legal compliance, validation profiles and electronic-invoicing network exchange may require additional rules or infrastructure.
