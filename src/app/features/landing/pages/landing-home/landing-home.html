FACTUR-X & ELECTRONIC INVOICING

What is Factur-X?

Factur-X is a hybrid electronic invoice format that combines a human-readable PDF with structured XML invoice data embedded inside the PDF. That means the recipient can read the invoice like a normal document while accounting and business software can process the structured data automatically.

x-facture provides a dedicated Factur-X creation workflow. When you choose Factur-X, the invoice is sent to the X-Facture API to generate the hybrid PDF instead of relying only on the browser's ordinary PDF export.

PDF for peopleA familiar invoice that can be opened and read normally.
XML for softwareStructured invoice data that can support automated processing.
Built for e-invoicingFactur-X is based on EN 16931 and the CII syntax.

What is a Factur-X invoice?

A Factur-X invoice is a hybrid electronic invoice: a readable PDF containing structured XML invoice data for automated processing.

Why use Factur-X instead of a normal PDF?

A normal PDF is primarily designed for people to read. Factur-X adds structured invoice data so compatible accounting and business systems can process invoice information more efficiently.

Does x-facture create Factur-X invoices for free?

Yes. Use the dedicated Factur-X creation workflow to generate a hybrid PDF containing a readable invoice and structured XML data, without installing desktop software or creating an account.

Can I download a normal PDF instead?

Yes. Invoice preview gives you two separate choices: a standard PDF download and a Factur-X download with embedded structured invoice data.

Does Factur-X apply to quotes and estimates?

Factur-X is an electronic invoice format, so it should not be presented as the format for ordinary quotes or estimates. x-facture keeps Factur-X tied to invoice-oriented workflows rather than labeling every business document as a Factur-X document.
